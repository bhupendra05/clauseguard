MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into between Acme Corp ("Company") and the Vendor.

1. TERM. This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and shall remain in force
for two (2) years, and shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless
terminated by the Company.

2. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed
under this Agreement. The confidentiality obligations shall survive in perpetuity.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. The Vendor hereby assigns all right, title and interest in any and
all work product to the Company, and all deliverables shall be deemed work made for hire.

4. INDEMNIFICATION. The Vendor shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company from any and all
claims, losses, and liabilities arising out of or relating to the services.

5. LIABILITY. The Vendor shall bear unlimited liability for any breach of this Agreement.

6. NON-COMPETE. During the term and for three (3) years thereafter, the Vendor shall not
compete with the Company in any market in which the Company operates.

7. EXCLUSIVITY. The Vendor shall act as the exclusive provider of such services to the Company
and shall not provide similar services to any competitor of the Company.

8. TERMINATION. The Company may terminate this Agreement at any time at its sole discretion,
with or without cause.

9. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and
any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration.
